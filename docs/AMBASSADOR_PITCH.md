### 2. Project Description (Short Essay / 150–200 Words)
> **Question:** *Describe your project, the problem it solves, and its core architecture.*

**Answer:**  
AurumAI is an open-source Market Intelligence Operating System engineered to eliminate behavioral bias and noise in macroeconomic analysis. Focusing on Gold (XAU/USD), US Inflation (CPI/Core CPI), Real Yields (FRED US10Y TIPS), and the US Dollar Index (DXY), it transforms raw economic releases into structured, explainable institutional assessments. 

Rather than functioning as a black-box trading bot, AurumAI executes a deterministic 14-stage Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) covering data ingestion, regime detection, counter-evidence auditing, volatility-adjusted position sizing, and institutional risk gating. The system enforces strict institutional rules (e.g., auto-holding signals below 60% confidence and penalizing structural trend conflicts) and computes distinct multi-timeframe pullback entry zones (H1/H4, D1, W1) with mathematically verified 1:2.00 Risk-to-Reward ratios. 

Operated 100% headlessly via GitHub Actions, AurumAI autonomously fetches daily market data, executes full DAG validation, verifies data lineage with SHA-256 locks, and delivers concise, institutional Arabic and English executive summaries directly to mobile desk channels via Telegram.

### 3. How AI / Codex Accelerated Development
> **Question:** *How did you use AI coding models (OpenAI Codex / LLMs) to build and scale this project?*

**Answer:**  
AI code-generation models were fundamental throughout AurumAI's lifecycle, functioning as an elite pair-programmer and accelerating development by an estimated 5x across four critical layers:
1. **Complex Pipeline Architecture:** Codex accelerated the scaffolding of our 14-stage DAG orchestrator (`src/orchestration/`), handling complex state dependencies and topological sorting.
2. **Rigorous Test-Driven Engineering:** Generating comprehensive automated test suites (18+ pytest suites), including out-of-sample temporal invariance, boundary testing for mathematical formulas, and mock fixtures for volatile financial APIs.
3. **Cryptographic Lineage & Integrity Locks:** Designing SHA-256 data fingerprinting algorithms (`knowledge/integrity/provenance.py`) that strictly lock data files against lookahead bias or unintended mutations.
4. **Real-time Multilingual Formatting:** Implementing edge templates in `src/operational_summary/` to automatically translate quantitative findings into natural Arabic institutional summaries for mobile traders.
