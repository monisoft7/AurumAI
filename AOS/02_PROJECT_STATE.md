# Project State

**File**: `02_PROJECT_STATE.md`
**Owner**: Technical Lead
**Update Frequency**: Updated whenever project state meaningfully changes. At minimum, reviewed weekly.
**Modification**: Any contributor may propose updates. Technical Lead approves.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document is the single source of truth for the current state of the project. It answers "where are we right now?" and provides enough context for anyone — human or AI — to understand what exists, what is active, what is blocked, and what has changed. It is the operational complement to `01_PROJECT_NORTH_STAR.md` (direction) and `04_ROADMAP.md` (future).

## 2. What Belongs Here

- Current phase or sprint identifier
- Summary of what is complete
- Summary of what is actively in progress
- Summary of what is blocked and why
- Key metrics and health indicators
- Recent significant changes (last 30 days)
- Links to relevant artifacts and documents
- Known risks and open issues

## 3. What Is Forbidden

- Detailed specifications or requirements
- Architectural decisions (belong in `03_ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY.md` and `05_DECISIONS.md`)
- Long-term plans (belong in `04_ROADMAP.md`)
- Personal opinions or evaluations of individuals
- Duplication of content from other AOS documents
