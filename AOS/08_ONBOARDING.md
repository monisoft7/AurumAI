# Onboarding

**File**: `08_ONBOARDING.md`
**Owner**: Engineering Lead
**Review Cycle**: Updated whenever the onboarding process changes, or at least quarterly
**Modification**: Engineering Lead approves. Contributors may suggest improvements via pull request.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document enables any new contributor — human or AI — to understand the project, set up their environment, and make their first contribution without relying on prior conversations. It is the entry point for everyone who joins the project.

## 2. What Belongs Here

- Reading order for AOS documents
- Environment setup steps
- How to get access to tools and resources
- How to make a first contribution
- Who to contact with questions
- Expected behavior and communication norms
- Common pitfalls and how to avoid them

## 3. What Is Forbidden

- Project-specific credentials, secrets, or access tokens
- Implementation details (belong in code and domain documents)
- Historical context that belongs in `09_CONTEXT.md`
- Strategic content that belongs in `01_PROJECT_NORTH_STAR.md` or `04_ROADMAP.md`

## 4. Reading Order

Every new contributor must read the following documents in order:

1. **`00_PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md`** — Understand the governing principles and how decisions are made
2. **`01_PROJECT_NORTH_STAR.md`** — Understand why this project exists and what success looks like
3. **`07_GLOSSARY.md`** — Learn the vocabulary used across all documents
4. **`03_ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY.md`** — Understand the design constraints and principles
5. **`06_ENGINEERING_RULES.md`** — Learn the standards and practices for contributions
6. **`09_CONTEXT.md`** — Understand the environment and landscape
7. **`02_PROJECT_STATE.md`** — Understand where we are right now
8. **`04_ROADMAP.md`** — Understand where we are going
9. **`05_DECISIONS.md`** — Understand the key decisions that shaped the project
10. **`10_NEXT_ACTION.md`** — Understand what needs to be done immediately

Total reading time: approximately 1-2 hours for a thorough first read.

## 5. First Contribution Path

### Step 1: Read the AOS
Complete the reading order above. Take notes on questions or concerns.

### Step 2: Set Up Access
Ensure you have:
- Read access to the project repository
- Write access to contribute (request from Engineering Lead)
- Access to any external tools or data sources listed in `09_CONTEXT.md`

### Step 3: Make Your First Contribution
Choose one of these starter tasks:
- Add a knowledge record to the domain knowledge base
- Fix an inconsistency in an AOS document
- Update `02_PROJECT_STATE.md` with current information
- Propose a term for `07_GLOSSARY.md`

### Step 4: Submit for Review
Follow the rules in `06_ENGINEERING_RULES.md` for submission and review.

## 6. Communication Norms

- All significant discussions must happen in writing (pull requests, decision documents, issues)
- Verbal or chat decisions must be recorded in `05_DECISIONS.md` within 24 hours
- Assume good faith — everyone is working toward the North Star
- Disagree with ideas, not people — evidence over opinion
- When unsure, ask — the only bad question is the one not asked

## 7. Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | How to Avoid |
|---------|-------------|
| Skipping the reading order | The documents are designed to be read in order; skipping causes confusion |
| Adding implementation details to AOS documents | If it is code or technology-specific, it belongs elsewhere |
| Making assumptions about terms | Check the glossary first — terms may have project-specific meanings |
| Working without checking `02_PROJECT_STATE.md` | The state may have changed since you last looked |
